# 02 — Concept Development

## ORDER / RUPTURE

### The Core Idea

The visual concept explores the tension between order and disruption.

ORDER represents structure, precision, organization and systematic thinking.

RUPTURE represents deviation, interruption, irregularity and the deliberate breaking of established visual patterns.

The relationship between these two principles forms the foundation of the project's visual identity.

### Connection to Design Practice

The concept reflects an approach to design that combines structured thinking with experimentation.

Architectural grids, systems and precise organization provide a framework. Deliberate deviations challenge that framework and introduce visual tension.

### Visual Exploration

The initial visual exploration was developed through an A0 poster in Figma.

The composition uses graphic elements, typography and spatial relationships to communicate the concept.

One example of deliberate disruption is the treatment of the word WASTE, whose size, position and rotation were changed to create a visual break within the composition.

### Translation into Digital Identity

The next stage is to translate the visual language of the poster into a single-page personal identity website.

The intention is to preserve the overall composition while introducing personal information and professional identity.

### Design Principle

The website should not look like a conventional business card or portfolio.

It should function as a unified visual composition in which personal identity and design thinking are communicated through the relationship between order and rupture.

---

*Concept development is an ongoing process. The visual language may evolve as the website is developed.*
